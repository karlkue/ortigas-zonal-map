# Ortigas Center BIR Zonal Value Interactive Property Map

An interactive, block-by-block GIS property map of the entire **Ortigas Center Central Business District (CBD)**, mapped across 6 dedicated Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) classification layers:

1. **Commercial Regular (CR)** - Commercial Land / Lots
2. **Commercial Condominium (CC)** - Office & Commercial Units
3. **Residential Regular (RR)** - Residential Land / Lots
4. **Residential Condominium (RC)** - Residential Condominium Dwelling Units
5. **Parking Slot (PS)** - Vehicle Parking Spaces (70% Statutory Rule)
6. **Institutional & Government (X / GL)** - Public, Educational, Religious & Utility Properties

## Jurisdictions & Department Orders Covered
- **Barangay San Antonio, Pasig City (RDO 43)** - D.O. 024-2023 (7th Edition)
- **Barangay Wack-Wack Greenhills, Mandaluyong City (RDO 41)** - D.O. 035-2021
- **Barangay Ugong Norte, Quezon City (RDO 39/40)** - D.O. 017-2022

## Key Features
- **613 Outer-Joined Property Polygons**: Complete district coverage across all blocks.
- **Dynamic Valuation Heatmap**: Color-graded legends that adapt in real time to the selected layer.
- **Property Inspector**: Click any building polygon to inspect rates across all 6 classifications.
- **Searchable Directory**: Instant search across 613 properties.
- **Transfer Tax Estimator**: Real-time computation of CGT (6%), DST (1.5%), and LTT (~0.75%).
- **Multi-Base Map Support**: OpenStreetMap, Esri World Imagery (Satellite), and CartoDB Positron.
