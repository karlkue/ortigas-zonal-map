Classification Code Definitions
CR (Commercial Regular): Commercial raw land parcels situated along commercial thoroughfares.
CC (Commercial Condominium): Individual commercial / office condo units in high-rise buildings.
RR (Residential Regular): Standard residential parcels situated along residential roads.
RC (Residential Condominium): Individual dwelling condominium units in multi-level towers.
PS (Parking Slot): Vehicle parking bays, assessed at 70% of the condo unit zonal value.
X / GL (Institutional / Government): Schools, churches, hospitals, and government reservations.
